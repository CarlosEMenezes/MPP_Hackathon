Pricing

Pay per call. Nothing else.

Every parameter is optional, so a bare call still returns something useful. Prices are in US-dollar stablecoins: pathUSD or USDC.e.

EndpointPriceParametersReturns
GET /api/search$0.01qMovies, shows and people matching the query. Trending today if empty.
GET /api/title$0.02q or type+idDetails, genres, runtime, rating, top 10 cast, directors or creators, US streaming / rent / buy.
GET /api/reviews$0.02q or type+id, limitAudience score, reviewer average and review excerpts.
GET /api/person$0.01q or idBiography, birthday, birthplace and top filmography.
